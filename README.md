### Behind the work

I'm Analice, a Full Stack Developer at **Bosch Service Solutions**. My work spans interfaces, backend systems, and cloud delivery. In my own products, I take that further: from the first design decision to deployment and maintenance.

I started in tech as an apprentice and grew into an analyst. Alongside my work, I'm pursuing an MBA in **Software Architecture** at Full Cycle.

### Things I've built

**[Worship Flow](https://github.com/analiceleite/worship_flow)** · *In use*

I conceived, designed, built, deployed, and maintain this product for worship teams, bringing songs, service schedules, roles, and reminders into one workflow.

One decision behind it: publishing a schedule requires server-side checks of the administrator's role and church context; email reminders are tracked separately through persisted notification attempts.

[Explore the demo](https://worship-flow-kappa.vercel.app/)

**[Crissia](https://github.com/analiceleite/crissia)** · *Working demo*

A management product I built for thrift stores, connecting inventory, sales, and shopping bags.

One decision behind it: protected server routes derive the store's tenant context from the authenticated user and explicitly scope privileged queries to that tenant, rather than trusting browser-side filters.

[Explore the demo](https://crissia.site)
